Race Log · Add a race

1 · Get the CSV

Open the race on the iRacing results page and use its export button. You get a file named eventresult_<subsessionid>_0.csv. This is the one step that can't be automated — downloading it needs your logged-in iRacing session.

2 · Drop it here

Drop CSV files here or click to choose — you can add a whole season at once

3 · Sign in

Publishing needs the team passcode — ask in the team Discord. It's the same for everyone and keeps the race log from being editable by the public.

4 · Publish

Publishing puts the races straight on the site — no commit, no deploy wait. Downloading is the escape hatch if you'd rather commit the file yourself.
